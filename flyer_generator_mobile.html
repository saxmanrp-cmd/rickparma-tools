Welcome to Gig Flyer Creator!
This is version one. Right now, the app creates a prompt you can copy and paste into your preferred AI, (ChatGPT, Claude etc). Just enter the basic gig details, and the app will do the rest.
Flyer Style & Theme
On the next page, pick a visual style like Cinematic, Playful, or Epic to set the overall look. You can also add a theme (like Star Wars or Toga) to give your flyer a creative twist.
Venue Website
For best results, include the venue's website. The AI can use it to find the venue's logo, colors, style, address, phone number, and website link.
No Required Fields
There are no required fields except for date and showtime. Not every gig is the same, so fill in only what applies to your event.
Artist Photos
This version does not support photo uploads yet. To add your photo, copy the prompt into your AI tool and upload your image there. If you have special guests, tell the AI which photo goes with each person.
