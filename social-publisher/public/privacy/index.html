Privacy Policy

Social Publisher

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Social Publisher is a private social-media management application used by its authorized operator.

Information processed

The app may process account identifiers and authorization tokens provided by connected social platforms, uploaded photos or videos, captions, scheduled post information, and publishing status data.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized operator, connect requested social accounts, store and schedule content, and send content to the social platforms selected by the operator.

Storage and service providers

Application data and media may be stored using Cloudflare services. Authorization tokens are stored in protected form. Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok process data according to their own policies when their services are connected.

Sharing and sale

Personal data is not sold. Data is shared only with service providers and connected social platforms as necessary to operate the requested publishing features.

Data deletion

The operator can disconnect connected social accounts and remove stored application data. Requests concerning this application can be sent to the contact address listed with the app in the relevant developer platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app's features or service providers change.
