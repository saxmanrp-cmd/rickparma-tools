About

Social Publisher is a private web application for creating, scheduling, and managing social media posts from one place.

The app connects authorized Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok accounts so the account owner can prepare media, captions, and scheduled publishing workflows.

TikTok content is uploaded through TikTok's Content Posting API for the creator to finish and publish in TikTok.
